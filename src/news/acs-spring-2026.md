The presentation highlighted the progress made in establishing a unified, FAIR-compliant repository for molecular dynamics biosimulation data across the Americas. With nearly 1,000 projects already integrated into the Florida Node, MDDB America is rapidly becoming an essential resource for the computational chemistry community.

**Key highlights from the presentation include:**

- Demonstration of the platform's capabilities for data discovery and access
- Overview of FAIR principles and their implementation in MDDB
- Plans for expansion across multiple institutions and regions
- Collaborative opportunities for researchers and organizations

This milestone represents a significant achievement for the molecular dynamics community and reinforces MDDB America's commitment to advancing open science and data sharing practices.

For more information about MDDB America and how to get involved, visit our [community page](community.html) or explore the [MDDB Florida Node](https://devmddb.rc.ufl.edu).
