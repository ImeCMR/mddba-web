    About MDDB America

    At MDDB America, we are developing a unified resource to organize and support molecular dynamics data from American research efforts - building on the initial European nodes that established the infrastructure.

    By improving access to these data, we aim to support reproducibility, secondary analysis, and scientific discovery in biochemistry, pharmacology, and personalized medicine.

    Our project addresses the need for an integrated platform that enables researchers to store, access, and build upon molecular dynamics data within the U.S. scientific community. We compile and organise all data generated by molecular dynamics simulations, making them accessible to drive new research.

    Our Vision | FAIR Data

    A Single Database for Molecular Dynamics Simulations

    Building on FAIR data principles for all MD simulation data.

    1. F

      Findable

      Persistent identifiers and rich metadata enable full discoverability of all simulation datasets.

    2. A

      Accessible

      Data retrievable via standardized, open, universally implementable protocols and APIs.

    3. I

      Interoperable

      Formal shared vocabularies supporting integration with the broader life sciences data ecosystem.

    4. R

      Reusable

      Rich provenance and clear licensing enabling long-term reuse and reproducibility.
